Cognitive Science & The "Cognitive Bump"

Traditional calorie trackers rely purely on mindless digital entry. FMCT stands out by implementing pre-scan mindfulness pauses and structured delay intervals. This psychological "Cognitive Bump" fosters conscious decision-making, helping users reflect on dietary choices before items are officially recorded.

How The Idea Came To Life

FMCT was born out of a desire to bridge physical microcontroller hardware with intelligent web analytics. By combining optical barcode scanning with cloud databases, the project transforms routine calorie tracking into an engaging, seamless scientific telemetry system.
